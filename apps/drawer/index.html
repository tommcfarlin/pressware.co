Questions.

How do I move an icon into the drawer?

Hold ⌘ and drag it between [ and ]. Drag it right of ] to keep it visible.

What happens to apps I open while it’s closed?

Their icons show right away. Only what was in the drawer stays hidden.

My Mac has a notch. Does it still work?

Yes. On macOS 26, Drawer tells you when icons won’t fit beside the notch.

The archive box disappeared. How do I get my icons back?

Open Drawer again from your Applications folder and the drawer opens.
