Drawer

Throw your menu bar icons into a drawer. Pull them out when you need them.

A drawer for your menu bar

Drawer adds a [ and a ] to your menu bar. Anything between them is in the drawer

Hold ⌘ and drag

Command-drag any icon between the brackets to put it away, or to the right of ] to keep it visible

One click to hide, one to show

Close the drawer and its icons disappear, leaving a small archive box. Click the box to bring them back

Notch aware

On a MacBook with a notch, Drawer tells you how many drawer icons fit beside it

Remembers where you left off

Drawer reopens open or closed, just the way you left it

Nothing to set up

No preferences, no permissions, no data collected, and no internet connection needed

Requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Free, signed with Developer ID, and notarized by Apple.
