Menu bar behavior

To hide icons, the App relies on how macOS lays out and draws the menu bar. On macOS 26 it changes the size of its own menu bar items; on macOS 27 it uses system features that Apple does not document. Apple may change either in a future release, and the App may stop hiding icons as a result.

On macOS 27 the App hides whole apps, so an app with one icon inside the drawer and one outside is hidden entirely while the drawer is closed. We are not responsible for icons that macOS hides or rearranges on its own, such as icons hidden by a notched display or a crowded menu bar.
