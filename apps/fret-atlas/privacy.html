Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 3, 2026

Overview

Fret Atlas ("the App") is an iOS application developed by Tom McFarlin ("we," "us," or "our"). This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version: We collect no personal data and never sell your information.

Information We Collect

The App collects and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your favorite chords — Chords you've saved for quick access
  • Your saved progressions — Custom chord progressions you've created
  • App preferences — Settings like left-handed mode

This data is stored locally using Apple's standard storage systems and is not transmitted to any server.

No Network Usage

The App operates entirely offline. It does not:

  • Connect to the internet
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Use any third-party APIs or services
  • Require an internet connection to function

All chord data, scales, and music theory content is bundled within the App itself.

Data We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your location
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Any personal information whatsoever

No Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, including:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools
  • Social media integrations

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. Since the App collects no data and requires no account creation, it is suitable for users of all ages.

Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all App data by:

  • Deleting the App from your device
  • Clearing favorites and saved progressions within the App

Since no data is transmitted to external servers, there is no server-side data to delete.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can view, modify, or delete your data at any time through the App's interface or by removing the App.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the App's practices, please contact us at support@pressware.co.
