What Is It?

Shelf Life helps you organize your books, track what you're reading, and figure out what to pick up next.

Build Your TBR

Add books by scanning a barcode, pasting an Amazon link, or entering details manually. Tag them as digital or physical, set your status, and get your To Be Read pile organized in seconds.

Stay Fresh

Every book on your shelf has a freshness score. They go from Fresh to Dusty to Expired the longer they sit unread. No more letting your TBR collect dust forever.

For You

Stuck in a reading slump? The For You tab gives you personalized picks from your own shelf based on your mood — quick reads, deep dives, genre mix, or surprise me.

Sell Your Shelf

If you have duplicates, books you won't reread, or simply DNF a book — it's time to sell and make space on your shelf for something new. Get instant resale quotes or generate QR codes for quick in-person sales.

Install the Beta

After you sign up, I'll send you an email invitation from Apple. Here's what to do when you get it.

1

Open the email on your iPhone

You'll receive an email from Apple with the subject "You're invited to test Shelf Life." Open it on the iPhone you want to test with.

2

Tap "View in TestFlight"

This opens Apple's TestFlight app. If you don't have it installed, you'll be taken to the App Store first.

TestFlight is a free app made by Apple for testing apps before they launch.
3

Install TestFlight (if needed)

If the App Store opens, tap "Get" to install TestFlight. It's free and takes about 30 seconds. Then go back to the email and tap the link again.

4

Accept the invitation

In TestFlight, tap "Accept" next to Shelf Life, then tap "Install." The app will download and appear on your home screen.

5

Open Shelf Life and add your first book

Tap the app to open it. You'll see a short walkthrough, then you can start adding books. Try scanning a barcode, pasting an Amazon link, or adding one manually.
