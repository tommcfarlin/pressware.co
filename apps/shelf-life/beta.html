What Is It?

Shelf Life helps you organize your books, track what you're reading, and figure out what to pick up next.

Build Your TBR

Add books by scanning a barcode, pasting an Amazon link, or entering details manually. Tag them as digital or physical, set your status, and get your To Be Read pile organized in seconds.

Stay Fresh

Every book on your shelf has a freshness score. They go from Fresh to Dusty to Expired the longer they sit unread. No more letting your TBR collect dust forever.

For You

Stuck in a reading slump? The For You tab gives you personalized picks from your own shelf based on your mood — quick reads, deep dives, genre mix, or surprise me.

Sell Your Shelf

If you have duplicates, books you won't reread, or simply DNF a book — it's time to sell and make space on your shelf for something new. Get instant resale quotes or generate QR codes for quick in-person sales.

What Happens Next

You don't need to do anything technical right now. Once the beta is ready, I'll personally reach out with an invitation and walk you through every step. Here's a quick preview of what that looks like.

1

I'll send you an email

When the beta is ready, you'll get a personal email from me along with an invitation from Apple to install the app. No action needed until then.

2

Install a free app called TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's official app for trying out apps before they launch. It's free and takes about 30 seconds to install from the App Store.

I'll include a direct link in your invitation email so you don't have to search for it.
3

Tap one link to install Shelf Life

The invitation email has a single link. Tap it, and TestFlight will handle the rest. Shelf Life will appear on your home screen just like any other app.

4

Open the app and start adding books

You'll see a short walkthrough when you first open the app, then you're ready to go. Scan a barcode, paste an Amazon link, or add a book manually.

5

Share your feedback

Use the app like you normally would and let me know what you think. I'll check in with you directly — no forms or surveys, just a conversation.
