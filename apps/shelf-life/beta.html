Shelf Life

We're looking for a few readers to help test the app before launch.

What Is It?

Build your TBR

Add books by scanning a barcode, pasting an Amazon link, or entering details manually. Tag them as digital or physical and get your To Be Read pile organized in seconds.

Stay fresh

Every book on your shelf has a freshness score. They go from Fresh to Dusty to Expired the longer they sit unread.

For you

Stuck in a reading slump? Get personalized picks from your own shelf based on your mood — quick reads, deep dives, genre mix, or surprise me.

Sell your shelf

If you have duplicates or books you won't reread, get instant resale quotes or generate QR codes for quick in-person sales.

What Happens Next

1

I'll send you an email

When the beta is ready, you'll get a personal email from me along with an invitation from Apple to install the app.

2

Install a free app called TestFlight

TestFlight is Apple's official app for trying out apps before they launch. It's free and takes about 30 seconds to install.

I'll include a direct link in your invitation email so you don't have to search for it.
3

Tap one link to install Shelf Life

The invitation email has a single link. Tap it, and TestFlight will handle the rest. Shelf Life will appear on your home screen just like any other app.

4

Open the app and start adding books

You'll see a short walkthrough when you first open the app, then you're ready to go. Scan a barcode, paste an Amazon link, or add a book manually.

5

Share your feedback

Use the app like you normally would and let me know what you think. I'll check in with you directly — no forms or surveys, just a conversation.
