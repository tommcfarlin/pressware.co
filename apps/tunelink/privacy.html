Privacy Policy

TuneLink

Last updated: February 2026

Pressware, LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TuneLink mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

TuneLink is designed with your privacy in mind. The App does not require account registration and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

When you use TuneLink to convert a music link, the following information is sent to our API:

  • The music URL you provide (a Spotify or Apple Music link)

This URL is processed solely to find the equivalent track on the other streaming service. We do not log, store, or associate these URLs with any personally identifiable information.

How We Use Your Information

Music URLs submitted through the App are used exclusively to:

  • Identify the track, album, or artist from the source streaming service
  • Find and return the matching content on the target streaming service

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the App's core conversion functionality.

Third-Party Services

TuneLink integrates with the following third-party services:

  • TuneLink API: Our backend service processes music URL conversions. The API receives only the music URL you submit and returns the converted link. No personal data is collected or stored by the API.
  • Spotify: Music links from Spotify are processed to identify tracks. TuneLink does not access your Spotify account or listening history.
  • Apple Music: Music links from Apple Music are processed to identify tracks. TuneLink does not access your Apple Music account or listening history.

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any other third parties.

Data Storage and Security

TuneLink does not store any user data on your device or on our servers. The App does not use local databases, cookies, or persistent storage of any kind beyond the standard iOS app preferences (such as whether you have completed the onboarding flow).

We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or any form of usage monitoring within the App.

Clipboard Access

TuneLink uses the iOS system paste button to access clipboard content. This requires your explicit action (tapping the Paste button) and is governed by Apple's clipboard privacy protections. The App only reads clipboard content when you actively choose to paste a URL. The share extension may copy converted links to your clipboard for convenience.

Children's Privacy

TuneLink is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email address below, and we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
