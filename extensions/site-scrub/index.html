    Install from a zip file.

    Site Scrub loads from a folder you download, in six steps.

    1. Unzip it and keep the folder.
    2. Open brave://extensions or chrome://extensions.
    3. Turn on Developer mode.
    4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.
    5. Pin the 🧽.
    6. Right-click it, choose Options, and pick your sites.

    Questions.

    Is it safe to install from a zip?

    The code is open to read, so you can check it first. You load the folder yourself, with no store in between.

    Why is it not in the Chrome Web Store?

    It is not listed there yet. The code is open to read in the meantime.

    Can I undo a scrub?

    No. Scrubbing is permanent, which is why the box always asks first.

    Will cookies sign me out?

    They can, if you turn on Advanced. Cookies and site storage can sign you out of those sites.

    Does it work in other browsers?

    It works in Brave and Chrome 102 or later. It runs on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

    How do I update?

    A dot on the icon and a banner on the settings page tell you when a new version exists. Download the new zip, unzip it over the old folder, and press reload.

    Scrubbing is permanent, and the extension always asks before it does anything.
