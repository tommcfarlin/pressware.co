Overview

Site Scrub (“the extension”) is a Manifest V3 extension for Brave and Chrome (Chrome 102 or later), developed by Tom McFarlin (“we,” “us,” or “our”). It permanently removes the browsing history of the sites you choose, with one click on the toolbar icon. This policy explains how the extension handles your information.

Information we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. Your history never leaves the browser.

How the extension works

Everything the extension does happens in your browser:

  • Finds and removes history. The extension reads your history through the browser’s history API only to find and remove the items for the sites you picked.
  • Asks before it scrubs. It shows a confirm box on the current tab only after you click the icon. This uses the activeTab and scripting permissions. On pages where a box cannot show, such as brave://history, it uses a system notification instead.
  • Checks for updates. A daily alarm runs the update check described below.

Data stored on your computer

The extension keeps the following in the browser’s local storage on your computer:

  • Sites you picked: the sites you chose to scrub.
  • Keep list: the sites you chose to protect.
  • Advanced switches: whether cookies, cached files, and site storage are included.
  • Update-check switch: whether Check for updates is on.
  • Newest version: the newest version number the extension saw.
  • Dismissed version: the version you dismissed with Later.

Nothing is stored on servers, no accounts are created, and no analytics are collected.

Permissions

The extension asks for these permissions:

  • History, to find and remove the items for the sites you picked.
  • Storage, to save your settings in your browser.
  • activeTab and scripting, to show the confirm box on the current tab after you click the icon.
  • Notifications, to show a system notification on pages where a box cannot show.
  • Alarms, to run the daily update check.

One optional permission, browsingData, is requested only when you turn on an Advanced option. It lets the extension remove cookies, cached files, and site storage for the sites you scrub. The extension asks for no access to the sites you visit.

Network usage

The extension makes one network request, about once a day. It is a plain read of https://pressware.co/extensions/site-scrub/version.json, a small static file that holds the newest version number.

  • The request carries no cookies, and the extension sends nothing about you.
  • The browser adds the extension’s id in the Origin header. Every copy of Site Scrub has the same id, so the id does not identify you.
  • Like any web request, the request shows your IP address to the web host.
  • We operate no server that receives data.

If you turn off Check for updates in the Advanced tab, the extension makes no network requests at all.

You can download the extension from GitHub Releases or from the Site Scrub page. GitHub’s own privacy policy applies to that download, and we do not receive any information about who downloads the extension.

Third-party services

The extension does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children’s privacy

The extension does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Because we don’t collect personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Your settings stay in your browser, and you can change them at any time from the extension’s settings.
