Description of the extension

Site Scrub is a browser extension that removes the browsing history of sites you choose, and, if you turn it on, cookies, cached files, and site storage for those sites. The extension is developed by Tom McFarlin (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

Requirements

  • Brave, or Chrome 102 or later.

Scrubbing is permanent

Removing history cannot be undone. Site Scrub keeps no copy of what it removes. Before it scrubs, the confirm box asks you to confirm, but once you confirm, the removal is final.

  • You are responsible for the sites you choose to scrub.
  • Counts shown in settings are estimates. The confirm box shows the exact number.
  • Keep protects a subdomain from being scrubbed.
  • Removing cookies or site storage can sign you out of sites.

Distribution and updates

The extension is a free download as a zip file from GitHub Releases or from the Site Scrub page. You load it in your browser’s developer mode.

Updates are manual. The extension tells you when a new version exists, and you download and load the new version yourself.
