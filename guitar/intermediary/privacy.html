Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what information Intermediary collects and what happens to it. It is written in plain language.

What I collect

Your purchase details, if you buy the workbook. The purchase happens on Etsy, not on this site. Etsy handles payment and shares order details with me, such as your name and email address, so I can support you. I never see your full card number. Etsy's own privacy policy applies to what it collects.

Basic visit information, such as which page you visited and where you came from, may be collected by this website's hosting and by any analytics I add. I use it to understand what is working, not to identify you.

The free lesson is a direct download. I do not ask for your email address and do not collect anything when you download it.

What I do with it

I use order details only to deliver the workbook and answer your questions. I do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

Etsy and the website host process your information under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can write to me to ask what I hold about you or to have it deleted.
